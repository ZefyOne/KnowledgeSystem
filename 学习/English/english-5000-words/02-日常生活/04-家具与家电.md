# 2.4 家具与家电 · Furniture & Appliances

### In My Room

My **bedroom** is my favorite **room**. There is a **bed**, a **desk**, a **chair** and a **wardrobe**. On the **desk**, there is a **lamp** and a **clock**. The **curtains** are blue, and there is a soft **carpet** on the floor. A **mirror** hangs on the wall, and a **picture** is above the bed.

In the **living room**, there is a **sofa**, a **table** and a **shelf**. The **TV** is on the wall. In the **kitchen**, we have a **refrigerator**, an **oven** and a **microwave**. The **washing machine** is in the bathroom. I **turn on** the **fan** when it is hot.

**整体大意**

卧室是我最喜欢的房间，里面有床、书桌、椅子和衣柜。书桌上有台灯和钟。窗帘是蓝色的，地上铺着柔软的地毯。墙上挂着镜子，床的上方挂着一幅画。

客厅里有沙发、桌子和书架，电视挂在墙上。厨房里有冰箱、烤箱和微波炉。洗衣机在卫生间。天热的时候我打开风扇。
