# 2.5 饮食与烹饪 · Food & Cooking

### Cooking Dinner

Tonight I **cook** **dinner** for my family. First, I **wash** the **vegetables** and **cut** them. Then I **boil** some **rice** and **fry** some **chicken**. I **mix** them with **salt**, **sugar** and **sauce**. The dish smells good and **tastes** **delicious**.

For **breakfast**, we usually **eat** **bread** and **eggs**, and **drink** **milk** or **coffee**. For **lunch**, we often have **noodles** or **rice** with **meat** and **vegetables**. We also eat **fruit** like **apples** and **bananas**. When we are **thirsty**, we drink **water** or **juice**.

**整体大意**

今晚我给家人做晚饭。我先把蔬菜洗好、切好，然后煮米饭、炒鸡肉，加上盐、糖和酱汁调味。这道菜闻起来很香，尝起来很美味。

早餐我们通常吃面包和鸡蛋，喝牛奶或咖啡。午餐常吃面条或米饭配肉和蔬菜。我们还吃苹果、香蕉之类的水果。口渴的时候，我们喝水或果汁。
