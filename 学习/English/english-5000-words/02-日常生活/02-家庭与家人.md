# 2.2 家庭与家人 · Family

### My Family

There are five people in my **family**: my **father**, my **mother**, my **brother**, my **sister** and me. My father is a doctor and my **mother** is a teacher. I am their **son**. My **brother** is older than me, and my **sister** is younger.

My **grandfather** and **grandmother** live in the countryside. They are my **parents'** parents. I also have an **uncle** and an **aunt**. Their **son** is my **cousin**. We are a big **family**, and we love each other very much.

**整体大意**

我家有五口人：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、妹妹和我。爸爸是医生，妈妈是老师，我是他们的儿子。哥哥比我大，妹妹比我小。

爷爷和奶奶住在乡下，他们是我父母的父母。我还有一个叔叔和一个姑姑，他们的儿子是我的堂弟。我们是一个大家庭，彼此非常相爱。
