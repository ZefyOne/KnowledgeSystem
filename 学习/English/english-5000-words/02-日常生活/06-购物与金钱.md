# 2.6 购物与金钱 · Shopping & Money

### At the Mall

On Sunday, I go to the **mall** to **buy** some things. I **pay** with my **card** because I don't have enough **cash** in my **wallet**. The **price** of the shoes is high, but they are on **sale**, so I get a **discount**. I also buy a **cheap** T-shirt.

I ask the **seller** about the **size** and **quality**. She gives me a **receipt**. I **spend** two hundred yuan. I want to **save** money, so I don't **buy** things I don't need. A good **customer** should be smart.

**整体大意**

周日我去商场买东西。我用卡付款，因为钱包里现金不够。那双鞋价格高，但在打折，所以我享受了折扣。我还买了件便宜的 T 恤。

我向售货员询问尺码和质量，她给了我收据。我花了 200 元。我想省钱，所以不买不需要的东西。好顾客应该精明。
