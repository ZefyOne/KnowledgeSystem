# 2.7 服饰与穿着 · Clothing

### Getting Dressed

In the morning, I **get dressed**. I **put on** a white **shirt** and black **trousers**. I **wear** a **jacket** because it is cold. I also put on my **socks** and **shoes**. My **belt** matches my shoes.

My wife likes to **wear** a **dress** or a **skirt**. She has many **coats** and **sweaters**. My son wears a **T-shirt** and **jeans**. In winter, we wear **hats**, **scarves** and **gloves**. These **clothes** are **comfortable** and **fit** well.

**整体大意**

早上我穿好衣服，穿上白衬衫和黑裤子。因为冷，我套了件夹克，又穿上袜子和鞋，皮带和鞋子很搭配。

我妻子喜欢穿连衣裙或半身裙，她有很多外套和毛衣。儿子穿 T 恤和牛仔裤。冬天我们戴帽子、围巾和手套。这些衣服很舒服，也很合身。
