# 2.3 房屋与居住 · House & Home

### My House

I **live** in a small **house** in the city. It has two **floors**. On the first **floor**, there is a **living room**, a **kitchen** and a **bathroom**. On the second **floor**, there are three **bedrooms**. Every **room** has a **door** and a **window**. The **walls** are white and the **roof** is red.

Outside, there is a small **garden** and a **garage**. The **gate** is blue. I **lock** the door with a **key** before I leave. My **neighbor** is very friendly. We **rent** this house from a **landlord**. I want to **move** to a bigger **apartment** next year.

**整体大意**

我住在城里一栋小房子里，它有两层。一楼有客厅、厨房和卫生间，二楼有三间卧室。每个房间都有门和窗，墙壁是白色的，屋顶是红色的。

外面有一个小花园和一个车库，大门是蓝色的。出门前我用钥匙锁门。我的邻居很友好。这房子是我们向房东租的，我想明年搬进更大的公寓。
