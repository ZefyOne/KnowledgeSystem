# 2.1 起居作息 · Daily Routine

### My Day

I **get up** at six and **wake** my son. I **wash** my face, **brush** my **teeth** and take a **shower**. Then I **dress** quickly and **eat** **breakfast**. I **leave** home at seven and **go** to **work** by bus. I **arrive** at the office at eight. I **work** all day and **finish** at six.

After **dinner**, I **relax** on the sofa and **watch** TV. I **feel** **sleepy** at ten and go to **bed**. I **fall asleep** quickly. This is my **daily** **routine**. It is simple but **regular**.

**整体大意**

我六点起床，叫醒儿子。我洗脸、刷牙、冲个澡，然后快速穿好衣服吃早饭。七点出门坐公交上班，八点到办公室。我工作一整天，六点下班。

晚饭后我在沙发上放松、看电视。十点我困了，上床很快入睡。这就是我的日常作息，简单但规律。
