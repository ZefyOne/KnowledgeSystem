# 8.3 季节天气 · Seasons & Weather

### The Weather Today

The **weather** changes a lot. Today it is **sunny** and **warm**. The **sun** is bright and the **sky** is blue. But tomorrow it will be **cloudy** and **windy**. The **wind** will blow hard.

In **summer**, the **temperature** is **hot**. Sometimes there is a **storm** with **thunder** and **lightning**. In **winter**, it is **cold** and **snowy**. **Snow** covers the ground. After the **rain**, we often see a **rainbow** in the sky.

**整体大意**

天气变化很大。今天晴朗又暖和，阳光灿烂，天空湛蓝。但明天会多云有风，风会刮得很大。

夏天温度很高，有时会有雷雨交加的风暴。冬天又冷又下雪，雪覆盖了地面。雨后我们常常能在天上看到彩虹。
