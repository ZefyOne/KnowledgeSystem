# 8.5 环境生态 · Environment & Ecology

### Protecting the Environment

We must **protect** the **environment**. Our **planet** faces many problems, like **pollution** and **global warming**. We should **reduce** **waste** and **recycle** paper, plastic and glass.

I **save** water and **energy** at home. I turn off the lights when I leave. I don't throw **garbage** on the ground. We should **conserve** natural **resources** and use **eco-friendly** products. If everyone helps, our **earth** will stay clean and green.

**整体大意**

我们必须保护环境。我们的星球面临许多问题，比如污染和全球变暖。我们应该减少浪费，回收纸张、塑料和玻璃。

我在家节约用水、用电，离开时关灯，不随地扔垃圾。我们应该节约自然资源，使用环保产品。如果人人都出一份力，我们的地球就能保持洁净和绿色。
