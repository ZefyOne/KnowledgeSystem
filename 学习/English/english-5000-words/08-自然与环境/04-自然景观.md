# 8.4 自然景观 · Natural Scenery

### Beautiful Scenery

My country has beautiful **landscape**. In the west, there are high **mountains** and green **valleys**. A big **river** flows through the **plain** and into the **sea**. Many **lakes** lie among the **hills**.

In the south, there is a long **coast** with sandy **beaches**. Tourists love the blue **ocean**. In the north, there is a dry **desert** and a dark **forest**. A tall **waterfall** falls from the **rocks**. I love the **scenery** of my country.

**整体大意**

我的国家风景优美。西部有高山和绿色的山谷，一条大河穿过平原流入大海，丘陵之间分布着许多湖泊。

南部有绵长的海岸线和沙滩，游客喜爱蓝色的大海。北部有干旱的沙漠和幽暗的森林。一条高高的瀑布从岩石上飞泻而下。我爱祖国的风光。
