# 8.2 植物 · Plants

### Plants in the Garden

I love **plants**. In my **garden**, I grow many **flowers** and **vegetables**. A big **tree** stands in the corner. Its **roots** are deep, and its **branches** spread wide. The **leaves** turn green in spring.

I **plant** some **seeds** and **water** them every day. Soon they **grow** into small plants. The **roses** **bloom** in summer. **Grass** covers the ground like a green carpet. I also grow **fruit** like apples and grapes. **Plants** make our world beautiful.

**整体大意**

我喜欢植物。在花园里，我种了很多花和蔬菜。角落里有一棵大树，根扎得很深，枝叶向四周伸展。春天叶子变绿。

我种下一些种子，每天浇水，它们很快就长成了小苗。夏天玫瑰绽放。草地像绿色的地毯一样铺满地面。我还种苹果、葡萄等水果。植物让我们的世界更美丽。
