# 8.1 动物 · Animals

### Animals Around Us

There are many **animals** in the world. Some are **pets**, like **dogs**, **cats** and **rabbits**. Some live on the **farm**, like **cows**, **sheep**, **pigs** and **chickens**. A **horse** helps people work.

**Wild** animals live in the forest. The **tiger**, **lion** and **bear** are strong. The **monkey** is clever, and the **elephant** is the biggest. Small animals like **mice**, **frogs**, **snakes** and **insects** are everywhere. I like to watch the **birds** and **butterflies** in the park.

**整体大意**

世界上有很多动物。有些是宠物，比如狗、猫和兔子；有些生活在农场，比如牛、羊、猪和鸡。马能帮人干活。

野生动物住在森林里。老虎、狮子和熊都很强壮。猴子很聪明，大象是最大的。老鼠、青蛙、蛇和昆虫等小动物随处可见。我喜欢在公园里看鸟和蝴蝶。
