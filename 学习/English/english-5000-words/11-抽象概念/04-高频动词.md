# 11.4 高频动词 · High-Frequency Verbs

### Actions Every Day

Every day, I **do** many things. I **get** up, **make** breakfast and **go** to work. At work, I **see** my colleagues, **say** hello, and **tell** them the news. I **use** a computer to **write** reports.

When I **come** home, I **help** my son with homework. We **watch** TV and **listen** to music. I **feel** happy when my family **stays** together. Before bed, I **read** a book. These simple **actions** make a full day.

**整体大意**

每天我做很多事：起床、做早餐、去上班。上班时见到同事，打个招呼，告诉他们新闻。我用电脑写报告。

回家后我帮儿子做作业，我们一起看电视、听音乐。家人聚在一起时，我感到幸福。睡前我读书。这些简单的动作构成了充实的一天。
