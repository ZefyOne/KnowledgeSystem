# 11.3 品质描述 · Abstract Qualities

### Important Qualities

Everyone has different **qualities**. My father teaches me the **importance** of **honesty** and **kindness**. My mother shows me **patience** and **courage**. These **values** are more important than money.

**Success** comes from hard work, and **failure** teaches us lessons. We should value **freedom**, **peace** and **justice**. A person's real **strength** is not **power**, but good character. **Wisdom** is better than **fame**.

**整体大意**

每个人都有不同的品质。父亲教导我诚实和善良的重要性，母亲则示范了耐心和勇气。这些价值比金钱更重要。

成功来自努力，失败教我们道理。我们应该珍视自由、和平与正义。一个人真正的力量不是权力，而是好的品格。智慧胜过名声。
