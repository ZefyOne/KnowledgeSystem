# 11.1 思维想法 · Thinking & Ideas

### Thinking and Ideas

I like to **think** about the future. I have many **ideas** in my **mind**. I **believe** that hard work brings success. I **remember** my teacher's words: "**Think** before you act."

When I face a problem, I **consider** the **reason** and the **effect**. I try to find the **truth** and understand the real **meaning**. My **goal** is to be honest and useful. I **hope** my **dreams** come true. Everyone should have a **purpose** in life.

**整体大意**

我喜欢思考未来，脑子里有很多想法。我相信努力会带来成功。我记得老师的话："三思而后行。"

遇到问题时，我会考虑原因和结果，努力寻找真相、理解真正的含义。我的目标是诚实、有用。我希望梦想成真。每个人都应该有人生目标。
