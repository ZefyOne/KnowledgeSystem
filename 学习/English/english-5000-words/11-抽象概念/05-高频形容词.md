# 11.5 高频形容词 · High-Frequency Adjectives

### Describing Things

We use **adjectives** to describe things. This box is **big** and **heavy**, but that bag is **small** and **light**. The **new** car is **fast**, while the **old** one is **slow**.

The **clean** room is **bright** and **quiet**. The **dirty** street is **dark** and **noisy**. These two apples look the **same**, but their taste is **different**. It is **important** to choose the **right** answer. Is it **possible**? Yes, nothing is **impossible**.

**整体大意**

我们用形容词来描述事物。这个箱子又大又重，那个袋子又小又轻。新汽车跑得快，旧汽车开得慢。

干净的房间明亮又安静，肮脏的街道黑暗又嘈杂。这两个苹果看起来一样，味道却不同。选对答案很重要。这有可能吗？有，没有什么是不可能的。
