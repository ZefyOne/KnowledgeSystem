# 11.2 程度数量 · Degree & Quantity

### How Much and How Many

We use **much** for things we can't count, and **many** for things we can count. I don't have **much** money, but I have **many** books. I need **more** time and **less** stress.

**Some** people like coffee, and **several** prefer tea. **All** of my friends are kind, but only a **few** live nearby. The **total** **number** of students is two hundred. The **average** age is twenty. Is that **enough**? Yes, we have **plenty** of food.

**整体大意**

不可数的事物用 much，可数的用 many。我没有很多钱，但有很多书。我需要更多时间和更少的压力。

有些人喜欢咖啡，有几个人更喜欢茶。我所有的朋友都很善良，但只有少数住在附近。学生总数是两百，平均年龄二十岁。够吗？够，我们的食物很充足。
