# 5.2 疾病症状 · Illness & Symptoms

### I Feel Sick

I **feel** **sick** today. I have a **fever** and a **cold**. My **head** **aches**, and I have a **cough**. My **throat** is **sore**. I **sneeze** a lot and feel **weak**.

My mother says I may have the **flu**. She tells me to stay in bed. A **headache** and a **stomachache** are common **symptoms**. If the **pain** doesn't go away, I should see a doctor. I hope I **recover** soon.

**整体大意**

今天我觉得不舒服，发烧又感冒。头很疼，还咳嗽，喉咙痛。我老打喷嚏，浑身没劲。

妈妈说我也许得了流感，让我卧床休息。头痛和胃痛是常见的症状。如果疼痛不消退，我就该去看医生。希望我能很快康复。
