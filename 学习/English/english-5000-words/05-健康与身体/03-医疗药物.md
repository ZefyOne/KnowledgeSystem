# 5.3 医疗药物 · Medical Care & Medicine

### At the Hospital

My grandfather is a **patient** at the **hospital**. The **doctor** **examines** him and gives him a **prescription**. I buy the **medicine** at the **pharmacy**. He takes two **pills** three times a day.

The **nurse** checks his **blood pressure**. If the **treatment** doesn't work, he may need an **operation**. The **surgery** is safe. An **ambulance** is ready for any **emergency**. The **dentist** is on the second floor. The **clinic** is clean and quiet.

**整体大意**

我爷爷是医院的病人。医生给他做了检查，开了处方。我在药房买了药，他每天吃三次、每次两片。

护士给他量血压。如果治疗无效，他可能需要做手术，手术是安全的。救护车随时待命应对紧急情况。牙医在二楼，诊所干净又安静。
