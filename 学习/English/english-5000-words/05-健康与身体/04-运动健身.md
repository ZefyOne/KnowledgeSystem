# 5.4 运动健身 · Exercise & Fitness

### Keeping Fit

I **exercise** every morning. I **run** in the park and **stretch** my body. On weekends, I go to the **gym** to **lift** weights and **train** my **muscles**. I also like to **swim** and **walk**.

Exercise makes me **strong** and gives me **energy**. When I **run**, I **breathe** deeply and **sweat** a lot. I want to **lose weight**, so I do more **sport** and eat less. **Fitness** is important for **health**.

**整体大意**

我每天早晨锻炼，在公园跑步、拉伸身体。周末去健身房举重、训练肌肉。我还喜欢游泳和散步。

锻炼让我强壮、充满精力。跑步时我深呼吸、出很多汗。我想减肥，所以多做运动、少吃东西。健身对健康很重要。
