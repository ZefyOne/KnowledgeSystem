# 5.1 身体部位 · Parts of the Body

### My Body

Our **body** has many parts. On my **head**, I have **hair**, two **eyes**, two **ears**, a **nose** and a **mouth**. My **eyes** help me see, and my **ears** help me hear.

I have two **arms** and two **hands**. Each **hand** has five **fingers**. I use my **hands** to write and eat. I also have two **legs** and two **feet**. Each **foot** has five **toes**. Inside my **body**, there is a **heart** and a **brain**. We should take care of our **body**.

**整体大意**

我们的身体有很多部位。头上有头发、两只眼睛、两只耳朵、一个鼻子和一张嘴。眼睛帮我看，耳朵帮我听。

我有两条胳膊和两只手，每只手有五根手指，用手写字、吃饭。我还有两条腿和两只脚，每只脚有五个脚趾。身体里面有心脏和大脑。我们要爱护自己的身体。
