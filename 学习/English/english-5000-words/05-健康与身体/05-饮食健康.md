# 5.5 饮食健康 · Healthy Eating

### Healthy Eating

A **healthy** **diet** is important. I eat a **balanced** **meal** every day: **fresh** **vegetables**, **fruit**, and some **protein** like fish and eggs. These foods are full of **vitamins** and **fiber**.

I try to eat less **fat**, **sugar** and **salt**. Too many **calories** make people **overweight**. **Organic** food is good for health. A good **diet** helps our body **digest** food and stay **slim**. We should eat well and live well.

**整体大意**

健康的饮食很重要。我每天吃均衡的一餐：新鲜蔬菜、水果，还有鱼、蛋等蛋白质。这些食物富含维生素和纤维。

我尽量少吃脂肪、糖和盐。太多热量会让人超重。有机食品对健康有益。好的饮食帮助身体消化食物、保持苗条。我们要吃得好、活得好。
