# 7.4 社交交往 · Social Interaction

### Making Friends

I like to **meet** new people. When I **meet** someone, I **greet** them and **introduce** myself. I **invite** friends to my home and **welcome** them warmly. We **chat** and **communicate** happily.

If I make a mistake, I **apologize** and my friend **forgives** me. Sometimes we **quarrel**, but we soon make up. I **keep in touch** with my old friends by phone. A good **friendship** needs trust and **contact**.

**整体大意**

我喜欢结识新朋友。见到人时，我打招呼并做自我介绍。我邀请朋友来家里，热情欢迎他们。我们一起聊天，愉快地交流。

如果我犯了错，我会道歉，朋友会原谅我。有时我们争吵，但很快和好。我和老朋友通过电话保持联系。好的友谊需要信任和联系。
