# 7.2 情感情绪 · Feelings & Emotions

### How Do You Feel?

People have many **emotions**. When I get a gift, I feel **happy** and **excited**. When I lose something, I feel **sad** and **disappointed**. Before an exam, I feel **nervous** and **worried**.

When I see a snake, I am **afraid** and **scared**. When I am alone, I feel **lonely**. My friend is always **calm** and **relaxed**. He never gets **angry**. I am **grateful** for his help. Our **mood** changes with our **feelings**.

**整体大意**

人有很多情绪。收到礼物时，我感到开心、兴奋；丢了东西时，我感到难过、失望。考试前，我感到紧张、担忧。

看到蛇时，我害怕、恐惧。独自一人时，我感到孤独。我的朋友总是平静又放松，从不生气。我很感激他的帮助。我们的心情会随着感受而变化。
