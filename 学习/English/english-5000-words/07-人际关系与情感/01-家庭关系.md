# 7.1 家庭关系 · Family Relationships

### Family Love

My **family** means a lot to me. We have a close **relationship**. My **parents** **care** about me and **support** my dreams. I **trust** them and **respect** them. We **depend** on each other.

When I was a **child**, my grandmother **looked after** me. Now I am an **adult**, but I still visit my **elderly** grandparents often. **Family** **love** never changes. We **share** joy and trouble together.

**整体大意**

家庭对我来说很重要。我们的关系很亲密。父母关心我、支持我的梦想。我信任他们、尊敬他们，我们彼此依靠。

我小时候奶奶照顾我。现在我是成年人了，但仍常去看望年迈的祖父母。家人的爱从不变。我们一起分享快乐，也一起分担烦恼。
