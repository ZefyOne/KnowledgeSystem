# 7.3 性格品质 · Personality & Character

### Different Personalities

People have different **personalities**. My sister is **kind** and **friendly**. She is always **polite** and **helpful**. My brother is **clever** and **brave**, but sometimes he is **careless**.

My friend Tom is **shy** and **quiet**, but he is very **honest** and **patient**. My teacher is **strict** but **generous** with praise. My uncle is **humorous** and makes everyone laugh. Everyone has both good and bad **qualities**.

**整体大意**

人有不同的性格。我妹妹善良又友好，总是有礼貌、乐于助人。哥哥聪明又勇敢，但有时粗心。

我的朋友 Tom 害羞安静，但很诚实、有耐心。老师很严格，但毫不吝啬表扬。叔叔很幽默，逗得大家笑。每个人都有优点也有缺点。
