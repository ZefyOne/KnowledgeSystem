# 7.5 恋爱婚姻 · Love & Marriage

### A Wedding

My sister is getting **married** this month. She fell in **love** with her **boyfriend** two years ago. Now they are a happy **couple**. He **proposed** to her last year, and she said yes.

The **wedding** will be in a garden. The **bride** will wear a white dress, and the **groom** a black suit. After the **wedding**, they will be **husband** and **wife**. They will celebrate their **anniversary** every year. **Love** makes life sweet.

**整体大意**

我妹妹这个月要结婚了。她两年前和男友相爱，现在是一对幸福的情侣。去年男友向她求婚，她答应了。

婚礼将在花园举行。新娘穿白裙，新郎穿黑西装。婚礼后他们就成了夫妻。他们每年都会庆祝结婚纪念日。爱情让生活变得甜蜜。
