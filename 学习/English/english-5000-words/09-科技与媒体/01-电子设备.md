# 9.1 电子设备 · Electronic Devices

### My Devices

I use many **electronic** **devices** every day. My **laptop** helps me work, and my **smartphone** keeps me connected. I have a **tablet** for reading and a **computer** at the office.

I use a **keyboard** and a **mouse** with my computer. The **screen** is big and clear. When the **battery** is low, I use a **charger**. I listen to music with my **headphones**. My **camera** takes good photos. **Technology** makes life easy.

**整体大意**

我每天使用很多电子设备。笔记本电脑帮我工作，智能手机让我保持联系。我用平板阅读，办公室里还有一台电脑。

我用键盘和鼠标操作电脑，屏幕又大又清晰。电池电量低时我用充电器。我用耳机听音乐，相机拍照效果很好。科技让生活变得轻松。
