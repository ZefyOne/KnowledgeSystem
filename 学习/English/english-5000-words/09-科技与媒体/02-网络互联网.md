# 9.2 网络互联网 · Internet & Network

### Going Online

I **go online** every day. I **connect** to the **wifi** and open the **internet**. I **browse** different **websites** and **search** for information. When I find something useful, I **download** it or **upload** my own files.

I have an **account** with a **username** and a **password**. I **log in** to check my **email** and **social media**. I **click** a **link** to read a **blog**. Sometimes I **chat** with friends in an online **forum**. The **network** is fast today.

**整体大意**

我每天上网。我连上 wifi，打开互联网，浏览不同的网站、搜索信息。找到有用的东西就下载下来，或者上传自己的文件。

我有一个账号，有用户名和密码。我登录查看邮件和社交媒体，点链接看博客。有时我在网上论坛和朋友聊天。今天的网络很快。
