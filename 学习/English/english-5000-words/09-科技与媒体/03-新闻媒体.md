# 9.3 新闻媒体 · News & Media

### The News

Every morning, I read the **news**. I read the **newspaper** and watch a **news** **program** on TV. The **headline** today is about the weather. A **reporter** **interviews** an expert.

The **radio** also **broadcasts** the news. A **magazine** **publishes** a good **article** about health. I like to watch a **documentary** on the science **channel**. The **media** tells us what is happening in the world. The **audience** trusts good **journalists**.

**整体大意**

每天早上我都看新闻。我读报纸，看电视新闻节目。今天的头条是关于天气的。一位记者采访了一位专家。

广播也播新闻。一本杂志刊登了一篇关于健康的好文章。我喜欢在科学频道看纪录片。媒体告诉我们世界上正在发生什么，观众信赖好记者。
