# 3.2 职业名称 · Jobs & Professions

### Jobs in My Town

There are many **jobs** in my town. My uncle is a **doctor**, and he works in a hospital. My aunt is a **teacher** at a school. My neighbor is a **policeman**, and his wife is a **nurse**.

My cousin is an **engineer**, and he builds bridges. His friend is a **lawyer**. My sister wants to be a **scientist**, and my brother wants to be a **pilot**. We also have a **farmer**, a **driver**, a **cook** and a **reporter** in our town. Every **job** is important.

**整体大意**

我们镇上有很多职业。我叔叔是医生，在医院工作；婶婶是学校老师；邻居是警察，他的妻子是护士。

表哥是工程师，负责建桥；他的朋友是律师。我姐姐想当科学家，哥哥想当飞行员。镇上还有农民、司机、厨师和记者。每一种职业都很重要。
