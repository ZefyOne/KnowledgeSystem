# 3.5 金钱财务 · Money & Finance

### Managing Money

I **earn** a **salary** every month. I put some **money** in the **bank** and keep some **cash** in my **wallet**. I have to **pay** **tax** and other **bills**. I make a **budget** so I don't **waste** money.

I want to **save** for a new car, so I **deposit** money into my **account** every month. I don't want to be in **debt**, so I never **borrow** too much. If I need more money, I can **borrow** from the bank, but I must pay **interest**. It is important to **manage** money well.

**整体大意**

我每月挣工资，一部分钱存进银行，一部分现金放钱包里。我得交税和各种账单。我做了预算，以免浪费钱。

我想存钱买新车，所以每月把钱存入账户。我不想负债，所以从不过度借钱。如果缺钱，我可以向银行借，但要付利息。好好理财很重要。
