# 3.1 办公职场 · Office & Workplace

### At the Office

I **work** in an **office**. My **job** is to write **reports**. Every morning, I turn on my **computer** and check my **email**. I have many **tasks** and **projects**. My **manager** gives me a **deadline** for each **project**.

My **colleagues** are friendly. We often have a **meeting** in the morning. I write notes on a **document** and **print** it with the **printer**. My **boss** is happy with my **work**. I want to build a good **career** in this **company**.

**整体大意**

我在办公室工作，我的工作是写报告。每天早上我打开电脑、查邮件。我有很多任务和项目，经理给每个项目定了截止日期。

我的同事们很友好，我们经常上午开会。我在文件上做笔记，然后用打印机打印。老板对我的工作很满意。我想在这家公司好好发展事业。
