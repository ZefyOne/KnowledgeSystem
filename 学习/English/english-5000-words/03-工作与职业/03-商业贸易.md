# 3.3 商业贸易 · Business & Trade

### Doing Business

My friend runs a small **business**. His **company** makes **products** for children. He **sells** his **goods** in the **market** and to big **clients**. He signs a **contract** with a **partner**. The **deal** is good for both sides.

He wants to **export** his **products** to other countries. He also **imports** some materials from abroad. His **business** makes a good **profit**, but sometimes there is a **loss**. He uses **advertisements** to **promote** his **brand**. Now his **industry** is growing fast.

**整体大意**

我朋友经营一家小生意，他的公司生产儿童用品。他在市场里出售商品，也卖给大客户。他和一个合作伙伴签了合同，这笔交易对双方都有利。

他想把产品出口到其他国家，也从国外进口一些材料。他的生意利润不错，但有时也会亏损。他用广告来推广品牌，现在他的产业正快速发展。
