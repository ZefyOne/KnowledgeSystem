# 3.4 会议沟通 · Meetings & Communication

### A Meeting

We have a **meeting** today. The **topic** is our new **project**. My **manager** gives a **presentation** and **explains** the **plan**. We **listen** carefully. Then we **discuss** the details. Everyone gives their **opinion**.

I **suggest** a new idea, and my **colleagues** **agree** with me. But one person **disagrees**, so we **argue** a little. At last, we **decide** to try my idea. The **manager** **confirms** the **decision**. We **arrange** the next **meeting** for Friday.

**整体大意**

今天开会，主题是我们的新项目。经理做演示并解释了计划，我们认真听。然后讨论细节，每个人发表意见。

我提了个新想法，同事们赞同，但有一个人不同意，我们小小争论了一番。最后我们决定试试我的想法，经理确认了这个决定，并把下次会议安排在周五。
