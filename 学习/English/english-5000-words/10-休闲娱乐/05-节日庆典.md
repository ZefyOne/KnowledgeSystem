# 10.5 节日庆典 · Festivals & Celebrations

### The Spring Festival

My favorite **festival** is the **Spring Festival**. It is the Chinese New Year. Our family **celebrates** it together. We put up red **decorations** and **lanterns**. Children get **gifts** and red envelopes with money.

On New Year's Eve, we have a big **party** and eat a big meal. At night, we watch **fireworks** in the sky. My son's **birthday** is also in winter. We light **candles** on a **cake** and sing a song. These **traditions** bring us joy.

**整体大意**

我最喜欢的节日是春节，也就是中国新年。我们全家一起庆祝，挂上红色的装饰和灯笼。孩子们会收到礼物和装着钱的红包。

除夕夜，我们举办盛大的聚会、吃丰盛的饭。晚上看天上的烟花。我儿子的生日也在冬天，我们在蛋糕上点蜡烛、唱歌。这些传统带给我们欢乐。
