# 10.4 游戏爱好 · Games & Hobbies

### My Hobbies

I have many **hobbies**. In my **free time**, I like to **play** **chess** and card **games**. I also enjoy solving **puzzles**. My son loves his **toys**, especially his **doll** and **kite**.

I like to **collect** stamps. My **collection** has stamps from many countries. **Hobbies** give us **fun** and **entertainment**. They help me **relax** and **enjoy** life. What is your **favorite** **pastime**?

**整体大意**

我有很多爱好。空闲时我喜欢下国际象棋、玩纸牌游戏，也喜欢解谜题。我儿子喜欢他的玩具，尤其是娃娃和风筝。

我喜欢集邮，我的藏品有来自许多国家的邮票。爱好带给我们乐趣和娱乐，帮助我放松、享受生活。你最喜欢的消遣是什么？
