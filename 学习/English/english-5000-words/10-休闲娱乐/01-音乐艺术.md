# 10.1 音乐艺术 · Music & Art

### Music and Art

I love **music**. I can **sing** and play the **guitar**. My sister plays the **piano**, and my brother plays the **drum**. We form a small **band**. Last month, we had a **concert** on the **stage**.

I also like **art**. I **draw** and **paint** **pictures**. My **painting** won a prize at the **exhibition**. The **museum** shows many beautiful **sculptures**. **Music** and **art** fill our life with beauty.

**整体大意**

我喜欢音乐。我会唱歌、弹吉他。妹妹弹钢琴，弟弟打鼓，我们组成了一个小乐队。上个月我们在舞台上办了场音乐会。

我也喜欢美术。我画画、涂色。我的画在展览上获了奖。博物馆展示了许多美丽的雕塑。音乐和艺术让生活充满美。
