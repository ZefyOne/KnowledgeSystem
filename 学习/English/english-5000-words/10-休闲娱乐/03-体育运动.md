# 10.3 体育运动 · Sports

### Playing Sports

I love **sports**. I play **football** with my friends every weekend. We are a **team**, and our **coach** teaches us skills. In a **match**, we try to **score** a **goal**. Last week, we **won** the game and got a **medal**.

My sister plays **tennis** and **badminton**. She also likes **swimming**. My brother plays **basketball** on the **court**. The **players** run fast. The **referee** watches the **game** carefully. **Sport** keeps us healthy and happy.

**整体大意**

我喜欢运动。每个周末和朋友一起踢足球。我们是一支球队，教练教我们技巧。比赛时我们努力进球。上周我们赢了比赛，还得了奖牌。

我妹妹打网球和羽毛球，还喜欢游泳。弟弟在球场上打篮球。球员们跑得飞快，裁判认真盯着比赛。运动让我们健康又快乐。
