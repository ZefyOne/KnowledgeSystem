# 10.2 电影电视 · Movies & TV

### At the Movies

On Friday, I go to the **cinema** to watch a **movie**. The **film** is a **comedy**. The **story** is funny, and the **characters** are lovely. The **actors** and **actresses** perform very well. The **director** did a great job.

At home, I like to watch a **TV** **series**. Each **episode** lasts forty minutes. My son loves **cartoons**. We also watch a **documentary** about animals. We eat **popcorn** while watching the **screen**.

**整体大意**

周五我去电影院看电影，那是一部喜剧片。故事很搞笑，角色很可爱，男女演员演得都很好，导演也很出色。

在家里，我喜欢看电视剧，每集四十分钟。儿子爱看动画片，我们还看关于动物的纪录片。我们一边看屏幕一边吃爆米花。
