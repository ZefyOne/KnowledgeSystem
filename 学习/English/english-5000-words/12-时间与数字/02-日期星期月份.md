# 12.2 日期星期月份 · Days & Months

### Days and Months

There are seven **days** in a **week**. From **Monday** to **Friday** are **weekdays**. **Saturday** and **Sunday** are the **weekend**. I like the **weekend** best.

There are twelve **months** in a **year**: **January**, **February**, **March**, **April**, **May**, **June**, **July**, **August**, **September**, **October**, **November** and **December**. My **birthday** is in **May**. I mark the **date** on the **calendar**.

**整体大意**

一周有七天。周一到周五是工作日，周六和周日是周末。我最喜欢周末。

一年有十二个月：一月、二月、三月、四月、五月、六月、七月、八月、九月、十月、十一月和十二月。我的生日在五月，我在日历上标注日期。
