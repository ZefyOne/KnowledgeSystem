# 12.1 时间表达 · Time Expressions

### Time

**Time** is precious. There are sixty **minutes** in an **hour**, and twenty-four **hours** in a **day**. A **week** has seven **days**, and a **year** has twelve **months**.

**Today** is Monday. **Yesterday** was Sunday, and **tomorrow** will be Tuesday. I get up **early** in the **morning** and go to bed **late** at **night**. **Soon** the weekend will come. **Finally**, I can relax. Don't waste your **time**.

**整体大意**

时间是宝贵的。一小时有六十分钟，一天有二十四小时，一周有七天，一年有十二个月。

今天是周一，昨天是周日，明天是周二。我早上起得早，晚上睡得晚。周末很快就到了，我终于可以放松了。不要浪费你的时间。
