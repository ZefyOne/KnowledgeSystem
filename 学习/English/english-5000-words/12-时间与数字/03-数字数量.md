# 12.3 数字数量 · Numbers

### Numbers

**Numbers** are everywhere. I can **count** from **one** to **one hundred**. **Ten** plus **five** equals **fifteen**. **Twenty** minus **five** equals **fifteen** too.

My class has **forty** students. The **first** student is the tallest. My seat is in the **third** row. A **thousand** people live in our town, and a **million** live in the city. **Numbers** help us understand the world.

**整体大意**

数字无处不在。我能从一数到一百。十加五等于十五，二十减五也等于十五。

我们班有四十名学生，第一名学生个子最高，我的座位在第三排。我们镇上住着一千人，城里住着一百万人。数字帮助我们理解世界。
