# 6.5 观光游览 · Sightseeing

### A Day of Sightseeing

During my **vacation**, I join a **tour** in the city. Our **guide** shows us the famous **attractions**. We go **sightseeing** and enjoy the beautiful **scenery**.

First, we visit a **palace** and an old **temple**. Then we go to the **beach** and see the blue **sea**. The **view** is wonderful. I take many **photos** with my **camera**. At the end, I buy some **souvenirs** for my friends. The **trip** is unforgettable.

**整体大意**

假期里我参加了一个城市观光团。导游带我们参观著名景点。我们观光游览，欣赏美丽的风景。

我们先参观了一座宫殿和一座古庙，然后去海滩看蓝色的大海，景色美极了。我用相机拍了很多照片。最后我给朋友们买了一些纪念品。这趟旅行令人难忘。
