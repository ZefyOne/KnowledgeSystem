# 6.1 交通工具 · Means of Transport

### Ways to Travel

There are many **vehicles** on the road. People go to work by **car**, by **bus** or by **subway**. Some ride a **bike** or a **motorcycle**. In the city, you can take a **taxi**.

For long trips, we take a **train** or a **plane**. A **plane** flies from the **airport**, and a **ship** or a **boat** sails on the sea. The **driver** drives the **bus**, and the **passengers** sit inside. **Traffic** is heavy in the morning.

**整体大意**

路上有很多交通工具。人们坐汽车、公交车或地铁上班，有些人骑自行车或摩托车。在城市里可以打车。

长途旅行我们坐火车或飞机。飞机从机场起飞，轮船或小船在海上航行。司机开公交车，乘客坐在里面。早上的交通很拥挤。
