# 6.3 地点场所 · Places & Locations

### Around the City

I live in a big **city**. There are many **places** to visit. Near my home, there is a **park**, a **museum**, a **zoo** and a **theater**. The **cinema** is next to the **restaurant**. The **bank** and the **post office** are on the main **street**.

Across the **bridge**, there is a **stadium** and a big **mall**. The **police station** and the **hospital** are in the center of the **city**. In the **country**, my grandparents live in a quiet **village**.

**整体大意**

我住在一个大城市，有很多地方可以逛。我家附近有公园、博物馆、动物园和剧院。电影院在餐厅旁边。银行和邮局在主街上。

过了桥，有一座体育场和一个大商场。警察局和医院在市中心。在乡下，我的祖父母住在一个安静的小村庄里。
