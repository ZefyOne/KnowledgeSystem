# 6.4 住宿出行 · Accommodation

### At the Hotel

We arrive at the **hotel** and go to the **reception**. I have a **reservation** for a **single room**. The **clerk** asks for my **passport**. I **check in** and get my **key** and a room **card**.

I take my **luggage** to the room. There is a **suitcase** and a small bag. I call **room service** to order dinner. Tomorrow I will **check out** before noon. The **fare** for the taxi is not expensive.

**整体大意**

我们到了酒店，走到前台。我预订了一间单人房。前台职员要看我的护照。我办理入住，拿到钥匙和房卡。

我把行李拿到房间，有一个行李箱和一个小包。我打电话叫客房服务订晚餐。明天中午前我会退房。打车费不贵。
