# 6.2 方向方位 · Directions & Positions

### Finding the Way

I am looking for the bank. A man tells me the **direction**. "Go **straight** and turn **left** at the **corner**. The bank is on the **right**, **opposite** the post office. It is **between** a bookshop and a coffee shop."

I walk **forward** and see a tall building. The bank is on the **north** side of the street. The **east** gate is closed, so I use the **west** gate. I find the bank at last. It is not **far** from my home.

**整体大意**

我在找银行。有人给我指路："一直走，到拐角左转。银行在右边，邮局对面，就在书店和咖啡店之间。"

我向前走，看到一栋高楼。银行在街道的北侧。东门关着，所以我走西门。最后我找到了银行，它离我家不远。
