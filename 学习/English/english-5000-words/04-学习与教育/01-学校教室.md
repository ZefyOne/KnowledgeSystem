# 4.1 学校教室 · School & Classroom

### My School

My **school** is big and beautiful. There is a **classroom** building, a **library**, a **gym** and a **playground**. Our **class** has thirty **students**. My **classmates** are friendly. Our **teacher** writes on the **blackboard** with **chalk**.

Every day we have many **lessons** and **courses**. We study different **subjects** like math and English. During the **break**, we play on the **playground**. The **bell** rings at eight, and the first **class** begins.

**整体大意**

我的学校又大又漂亮，有教学楼、图书馆、体育馆和操场。我们班有 30 名学生，同学们都很友好。老师用粉笔在黑板上写字。

每天我们有很多课，学习数学、英语等不同科目。课间休息时，我们在操场上玩。八点铃声响起，第一节课开始。
