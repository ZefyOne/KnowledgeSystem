# 4.4 考试评价 · Exams & Evaluation

### Preparing for the Exam

We will have an **exam** next week. I **study** hard and **review** my notes every day. I **memorize** new words and try to **remember** the important points. Some questions are **difficult**, but I try not to make a **mistake**.

On the day of the **exam**, I read every **question** carefully and write the **answer** on the answer sheet. After the exam, I get my **score**. I **pass** the exam with a high **grade**. My **result** is good, and my **knowledge** has improved a lot.

**整体大意**

下周我们要考试。我每天努力学习、复习笔记，背生词、记重点。有些题很难，但我尽量不犯错。

考试当天，我仔细读每道题，把答案写在答题卡上。考完后我拿到了分数，以高分通过了考试。成绩不错，我的知识也进步了很多。
