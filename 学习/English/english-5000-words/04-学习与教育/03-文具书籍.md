# 4.3 文具书籍 · Stationery & Books

### My Schoolbag

I have a heavy **schoolbag**. Inside, there are many **books**: my **textbooks**, a **dictionary** and some **notebooks**. I write with a **pen** or a **pencil**. When I make a mistake, I use an **eraser**. I draw lines with a **ruler**.

I also carry **paper**, **glue**, **scissors** and some **crayons**. My favorite **book** is a **novel** about a boy. Its **author** is famous. The **story** is very interesting. I read a **magazine** in the library after school.

**整体大意**

我的书包很重，里面有很多书：课本、词典和几本笔记本。我用钢笔或铅笔写字，写错了就用橡皮擦，画线用尺子。

我还带着纸、胶水、剪刀和几支蜡笔。我最喜欢的书是一本关于一个男孩的小说，作者很有名，故事很有趣。放学后我在图书馆看杂志。
