# 4.2 学科科目 · Subjects

### My Favorite Subject

I study many **subjects** at school. My favorite **subject** is **English**. I also like **math** and **science**. **Science** includes **physics**, **chemistry** and **biology**. My sister likes **history** and **geography**. My brother is good at **music** and **art**.

We have **physical education** (P.E.) twice a week. We also learn **computer** science. **Chinese** is our own **language**, and English is a **foreign** language. Every **subject** is useful in its own way.

**整体大意**

我在学校学很多科目，最喜欢的是英语，也喜欢数学和科学。科学包括物理、化学和生物。姐姐喜欢历史和地理，哥哥擅长音乐和美术。

我们每周上两次体育课，还学计算机。中文是我们的母语，英语是外语。每一门科目都有它自己的用处。
